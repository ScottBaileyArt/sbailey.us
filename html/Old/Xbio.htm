For more than a decade, Scott Bailey has held solo exhibitions in the US, Japan, Italy, Egypt, and South Africa, and his work has been critically recognized in a variety of national and international publications. Bailey is also a critic, curator, and educator who currently writes as the Seattle Corresponding Editor for Contemporary Magazine (London) and serves as the head of the Art Department at Wenatchee Valley College.
