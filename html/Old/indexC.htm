Virtually Sublime questions whether it is possible to achieve the painter’s notion of the sublime in the landscape through contemporary technology, utilizing satellite imaging as inspiration for paintings that are repeatedly fragmented and re-assembled through digital mapping and painting processes. The installation includes a painting that covers the entire 16'x53' west wall of Sarah Spurgeon Gallery with a view of Mt. Rainier, located 60 miles to the west. A second piece involves a live projection of a painting of a satellite image of Mt. Rainier onto a white, scale model of the mountain.
